## Case design language

**Proportions (stated dims only, never estimated).** Slim aluminium consensus: **L:W:H ≈ 5.5–5.8 : 2.1–2.2 : 1** (Hifot 5.85:2.19:1 · Philley 5.58:2.15:1 · Boolavard 5.57:2.14:1). Premium leather thin ceiling: Ettinger Capra 5.53:2.17:1 at 3 cm. Designer bulk baseline: Garrett Leight 3.69:1.56:1 at 45.5 mm. **EyeQ 3D target (from Boolavard, exact mm): 156 × 60 × 28 mm**, sculpted *down* from the box: taper ends, crown lid ~2–3 mm, edge radii ≥ 4 mm.

**Materials.** Matte anti-slip-coated aluminium alloy (Boolavard) · stainless shell under PU leather + elastic mesh (SpecNest) · oil-finished Japanese leather (Mykita/Diffuser) · goat leather + suede (Ettinger) · crossgrain calf + suede (Smythson). Warning: Oakley's "carbon" is a printed look-skin over alloy — never spec "carbon fiber" without a real layup supplier.

**Parting lines / flap types (observed, nothing invented).** (1) Snap clamshell, full-perimeter line + hinge + snap (Hifot/Philley/Boolavard) — reliable but reads as two boxes. (2) Zip clamshell (Oakley) — sporty, bulky, rejected. (3) Popper foldover flap + nosepiece (Ettinger) — closest working flap. (4) Open slip sleeve, zero hinge (JMM Circa) — closest continuous shell. (5) Lidded box (Mykita/Smythson/GLCO). (6) Elastic mesh cradle inside clamshell (SpecNest) — retention without foam.

**Concrete recommendations for the THIN aerodynamic one-piece-flap case (brief §6):**
1. **Proportion:** 156 × 60 × 28 mm start, stretched ≈ 165 × 62 × 26 mm and sculpted — ogive/leaf plan taper (max width ~40% from hinge end), crowned lid, knife-edge parting hidden in the shadow line; visual ratio ≈ 6.3 : 2.4 : 1.
2. **Shell:** near-black graphite, matte with subtle dark metallic flake (Philley Gun Grey + Smythson crossgrain register); **single continuous outer skin — no full-perimeter clamshell split**; one longitudinal shadow groove on the flank, flap edge feathering to ~1.2 mm.
3. **Flap:** one-piece continuous shell (Ettinger foldover × JMM sleeve logic, rigidized): living-hinge zone thinned from inside, smooth slightly-elastic arc (never a 90° box lid); magnetic catch buried under lining, no visible snap; dark-red velvet over a nosepiece saddle so glasses self-seat.
4. **Logo:** physically embossed/debossed geometry per brief — JMM's Art-Deco deboss + 24k arrowhead proves hardware-level branding beats decals; EyeQ logo debossed ~0.4 mm into the lid crown + "EyeQ Vision Care" micro-engraved on the flank (never floating text, never glow).
5. **Reject:** zip tracks, visible piano hinges, two-box clamshell symmetry, carbon-look vinyl, contrast stitching.

## Velvet reference (what makes velvet read as velvet under light)

Three verified sources, one physics story:

1. **Blender Principled BSDF manual** — `https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/shader_nodes/shader/principled.html` — verbatim: *"Sheen simulates very small fibers on the surface. For cloth this adds a soft velvet like reflection near edges,"* controls Weight 0–1 / Roughness / Tint, *"sheen layer sits on top of all other layers, to add fuzz or dust."* → **velvet = dark diffuse base + bright grazing retro-reflection (fresnel-edge sheen)**; flat front light kills it, rim/grazing light makes it.
2. **V&A real velvet** — search `https://collections.vam.ac.uk/search/?page=1&page_size=15&q=velvet` (7,119 objects) · object `https://collections.vam.ac.uk/item/O93220/velvet-unknown/` (`screens/E/E-12-vam-velvet-O93220.png`, late-16th-c. panel) → **pile-direction nap = light/dark bands; crushed pile = sharp highlight-to-shadow cutoff** — the lighting-angle and fold-placement reference.
3. **Khronos glTF sheen spec** — `https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF/tree/main/extensions/2.0/Khronos/KHR_materials_sheen` → web-safe velvet = `sheenColor + sheenRoughness + sheenColorTexture`; maps 1:1 to Blender sheen for WebGL moments.
4. Holland & Sherry velvet page **UNVERIFIED** — `https://www.hollandandsherry.com/` homepage exists but no velvet path found (guessed `/en/collections/velvet` → 404). Dormeuil / Loro Piana velvet pages **UNVERIFIED**.

**Recipe for EyeQ dark-red velvet (Phase 3/4 handoff):** near-black-red diffuse base (#2a0608-ish, tune on render) + high sheen weight, high sheen roughness, deep-red sheen tint; key light grazing across folds; **fold geometry (not normal maps)** carries the read; rim light mandatory in darkness scenes — without it velvet collapses to black felt (Moscot flat-lay E-07 works *because* of raking light).

## Motion/pacing principles (observed scroll cinema → brief §5 film)

Pacing weights: case 10 · open/velvet 15 · emerge 15 · unfold 15 · info+camera 15 · wave1 15 · wave2 7 · lens approach 5 · lens entry/white 3 (dark ≈ 80%, white ≈ 20%).

1. **Chapter-per-benefit beats (AirPods E-11):** one idea per viewport, sticky 3D stage + text crossfade; each film beat = one pinned scene with its own caption; never two reveals competing.
2. **Immersion-slider portal (Vision Pro E-12):** lens approach/entry is a *continuous blend* (opacity + scale), not a cut — scrub 0.8–1.4, `ease: "none"`, scroll position is the timing.
3. **Iris reveal (AirPods ripple E-29):** open/velvet beat uses `clip-path: circle()` expansion; lens entry inverts it (circle contracts to white).
4. **Marquee + clip-wipe mechanics (Realevate E-30):** duplicated-track brand rails; `inset()` wipes for section entries — cheap, 60 fps, no WebGL needed for rails.
5. **Preloader → hero curtain (playbook R2):** counter 0→100, clip-path curtain, SplitText lines up; hero readable ≤ 1.8 s; reduced-motion = final states, no scrub/pin/smooth-scroll.
6. **Motion tokens (cinematic-scroll-storytelling):** enter `power3/4.out`; scrubbed scenes linear; text 0.8–1.1 s, line stagger 0.08–0.14, word stagger 0.035–0.07; reveal offset y 24–48; parallax speeds ≤ ±0.18; sticky recede to 0.92 scale.
7. **Restraint law (design-motion-principles):** rare-frequency hero (seen ~once/visit) may be expressive; booking/practical UI is frequent — instant, no theater; keyboard-initiated = never animate.
8. **Slow film over fast 3D:** every eyewear reference drifts slowly — except ic! berlin's motorsport register, which EyeQ rejects. Hyperspace sponsor waves = velocity in *depth* (z-flight), not shaky cam.

## Anti-patterns to avoid (AI slop list — audit-ai-design-slop classes)

1. **P0 — Fake proof:** invented testimonials, ratings, brand logos, "award-winning" claims. Sponsor waves render only research-verified identities (lane C owns facts).
2. **P0 — Modal stack blocking the hero** (observed: Gentle Monster cookie+SMS, Cubitts newsletter+cookie, Moscot SMS popup, ic! berlin relaunch popup, Cartier triple-stack). EyeQ: zero popups in the first three viewports.
3. **P1 — One-word hype text** (VISION/FOCUS/CLARITY) — brief §7 bans it; luxury sites use two-word chapter titles ("A LIGHT TOUCH", "PURE SHAPES", "TONAL SHIFTS") or engineering poetry ("From 0.5 mm to 300 km/h").
4. **P1 — Gradient blobs / glassmorphism / particle fields as "cinema"** — zero occurrences across all luxury/cinematic references; darkness comes from lighting (raking key, rim, falloff), never purple gradients.
5. **P1 — Motion theater:** unpausable carousels, scroll-hijack, readability-harming parallax, missing `prefers-reduced-motion` (ic! berlin pause/mute = counter-pattern to copy).
6. **P2 — Template giveaways:** Inter-as-display without reason, default bento grids, icon-tile rows, card on every block, centered long paragraphs, nested rounded containers communicating no hierarchy.
7. **P2 — Copying identity:** JMM quirk, Gentle Monster weirdness, Cubitts twee voice, Moscot yellow/black shouting, Oakley ballistic styling, Apple Buy-bar — study choreography, never lift look/voice.
8. **P2 — Fake materials** in 3D (Oakley E-24 lesson): honest PBR only, HDRI on every material, metals reflect, never flat black.
9. **P3 — Competing ideas per viewport:** split-hero indecision, two display faces, three equal CTAs. One viewport = one focal point = one primary action.
